How to Play L'Attaque

Objective

Capture your opponent's Flag or eliminate all their movable pieces.

Setup

Place your 40 pieces on your side of the board (rows closest to you). Use Randomize for a quick layout, or drag pieces to arrange them yourself. Click Start Game when ready.

Movement

  • Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move.
  • Most pieces move one square horizontally or vertically (no diagonals).
  • Scouts (rank 2) can move any number of empty squares in a straight line.
  • Bombs and the Flag cannot move.
  • The two lakes in the center are impassable.

Combat

  • Move onto an enemy piece to attack. Higher rank wins.
  • Equal ranks: both pieces are removed.
  • Spy defeats the Marshal only when the Spy initiates the attack.
  • Miners defuse Bombs and survive. All other pieces are destroyed by Bombs.

Tips

  • Protect your Flag with Bombs.
  • Keep Miners alive — they're the only way to clear Bombs.
  • Use Scouts to probe enemy positions from a distance.
  • Guard your Marshal from the Spy.
